Beat-HAR architecture — HAR ⊕ deep forecast combination (VN30 Parkinson variance, horizon t+h)

The single deep model does not reliably beat HAR out-of-sample. The forecast that beats HAR is a fixed, untuned equal-weight combination of the classical HAR forecast and the deep forecast on the variance scale, floored for positivity. Combining works because HAR and the non-linear model make partially uncorrelated errors (Bates–Granger). Diebold–Mariano vs HAR (QLIKE, seed-ensembled): h1 p<0.001, h5 p<0.001 (5-seed robust), h10 tie, h22 HAR best.
