F. Conclusion

Earnings helps S&P 500 volatility forecasting for two compounding reasons, both visible above at h1, and each is the mirror image of the HOSE failure. (1) Comprehensive coverage. About 9.3% of test observations sit within the earnings window, because US firms report on scheduled quarterly dates for the whole panel (versus only ~5.6% of the HOSE test set, where the crawled Vietnamese dates are sparse). So GBM+earn can differ from GBM on a large, informative slice of the test set rather than being numerically identical to it. (2) A real, un-damped around-announcement vol spike. Where the feature fires, the realized-vol ratio of proximate vs baseline days is ~1.60 -- clearly above 1 -- so there is a genuine earnings volatility jump to lock onto, unlike HOSE's ~1.1 muted response, where daily price-limit bands damp the informational jump. Consequently, on the subset where the earnings feature actually moves the prediction, it HELPS about 67% of the time (HOSE hurts ~68%): with a genuine vol response to exploit, the extra features add signal rather than variance. In short, the success is 'comprehensive dates to fire on' AND 'a real, limit-free announcement vol spike', exactly the two ingredients HOSE lacks.
