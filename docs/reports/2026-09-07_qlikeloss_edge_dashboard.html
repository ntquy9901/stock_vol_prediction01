QLIKE-loss: where and why the deep + graph edge fades at h5/h10/h22

Per-cell (stock×day) test forecasts, QLIKE-loss training (anchor=none). Deciles D0..D9 = cells sorted by that day's realized volatility (D0 calmest, D9 stormiest). Panels available: sp500_clean_h1, sp500_clean_h10, sp500_clean_h22, sp500_clean_h5, vn100_h1, vn100_h10, vn100_h22, vn100_h5, vn30_h1, vn30_h10, vn30_h22, vn30_h5.

1. Headline: QLIKE by horizon

Deep (LSTM/VolGA, QLIKE-trained) beat HAR-X at h1 on all three panels. At longer h the lines converge (VN) or stay below (SP500).

2. THE ISSUE — LSTM vs HAR-X by decile

At h1 LSTM wins across deciles. At h5/h10/h22 it wins only the calm deciles (D0-D3) and LOSES the volatile ones (D5-D9) — the losses cancel the calm wins, so it no longer beats HAR. On SP500 the calm win is big enough to stay net-negative (wins at h22); on VN it cancels to ~0.

3. GRAPH VALUE — VolGA vs LSTM by decile

The volume→Parkinson graph helps a little at h1 but adds NOISE on the storm decile (D9) at h5/h10/h22; the net graph value collapses to ~0 at longer horizons.

4. Why calm cells dominate

QLIKE is far larger on the calm deciles (D0-D2), so the deep model's durable skill there drives the whole mean — which is why the calm-cell edge decides significance.
