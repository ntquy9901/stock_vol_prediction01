7. Verdict

  • The vol→PK graph is a statistically significant marginal contributor over the no-graph LSTM at short horizons (h1 p=0.008, h5 p=0.011) and not at longer horizons (h10 p=0.229, h22 p=0.107), consistent with volatility-spillover literature in which cross-asset transmission is strongest at short lead times.
  • No deep model (LSTM or VolGA) significantly outperforms HAR-X at any horizon (VolGA vs HAR-X p = 0.177, 0.585, 0.520, 0.842 for h1/h5/h10/h22).
  • On point QLIKE, HAR-X wins at h5, h10, h22; VolGA wins at h1. LSTM is statistically indistinguishable from HAR-X (equivalence) at every horizon.
  • Walk-forward protocol: expanding window, 22 monthly-retrain folds, per-fold train-only graph construction and feature scalers, date-clustered Diebold-Mariano inference.

Data provenance: clean enriched VN100 panel (data/processed_enriched/vn100), Parkinson-variance target (σ², not σ), QLIKE positivity floor 1e-08; training config epochs=16, patience=5, min_epochs=5, batch_size=32; 5 seeds. Source JSONs: results/walkforward_volga/walkforward_volga_vn100_h{1,5,10,22}.json.
