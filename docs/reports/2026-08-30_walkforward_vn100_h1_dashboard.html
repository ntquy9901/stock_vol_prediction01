1. How the data is organised over time (train / val / forecast)

Expanding-window walk-forward, retrain every K=66 days. Per fold: train on ALL history up to the retrain point (expanding), early-stop on the last val=66 days behind a purge=h gap, freeze, forecast the next 66 days. 7 forecast blocks tile the 454-date OOS region (= the fixed-split test set, 102 nodes). No forecast date ever enters any train/val window (asserted leakage guard).
