F. Conclusion

Earnings does not help HOSE volatility forecasting for two compounding reasons, both visible above at h1. (1) Little to fire on. Only 5.6% of test observations sit within the earnings window at all; this is 13.0% in the dense post-2025 crawl window but only 0.8% before it, so GBM+earn is numerically identical to GBM on the large majority of the test set and cannot move the pooled metric. (2) Muted around-announcement vol response. Even where the feature does fire, the realized-vol ratio of proximate vs baseline days is ~1.08 -- HOSE volatility around scheduled announcements is essentially indistinguishable from the baseline, unlike the pronounced announcement spike on the S&P 500 that GBM+earn exploits there. Vietnamese daily price-limit bands damp the informational jump that a US earnings surprise produces. Consequently, on the subset where the earnings feature actually moves the prediction, it hurts about 68% of the time: with no genuine vol response to lock onto, the extra features mostly add variance. In short, the failure is 'no data to fire on' AND 'fires but the vol response is muted / limit-damped', not a coverage-only artefact.
