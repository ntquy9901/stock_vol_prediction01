7. Verdict

  • On VN30 (31 nodes) the vol→PK graph is not a statistically significant marginal contributor over the no-graph LSTM at any horizon (h1 p=0.179, h5 p=0.112, h10 p=0.265, h22 p=0.928).
  • No deep model (LSTM or VolGA) significantly outperforms HAR-X at any horizon (VolGA vs HAR-X p = 0.157, 0.397, 0.717, 0.788 for h1/h5/h10/h22).
  • On point QLIKE, HAR wins at h1, HAR-X wins at h5, h10, h22; no deep model wins any horizon.
  • Contrast with VN100 (102 nodes), where the same graph is a significant marginal contributor at h1 (p=0.008) and h5 (p=0.011): graph value tracks node breadth and liquidity rather than correlation magnitude alone.
  • Walk-forward protocol: expanding window, 22 monthly-retrain folds, per-fold train-only graph construction and feature scalers, date-clustered Diebold-Mariano inference.

Data provenance: clean enriched VN30 panel (data/processed_enriched/vn30), Parkinson-variance target (σ², not σ), QLIKE positivity floor 1e-08; training config epochs=16, patience=5, min_epochs=5, batch_size=32; 5 seeds. Source JSONs: results/walkforward_volga/walkforward_volga_vn30_h{1,5,10,22}.json.
