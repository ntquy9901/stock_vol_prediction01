3. Robustness of the marginal cells (3-seed → 5-seed)

Cells near p=0.05 were re-estimated at 5 seeds. The one apparent "graph beats HAR" result (graphical-LASSO @ h5) collapses; the LSTM-nonlinearity result strengthens — confirming the value is nonlinearity over the node features, not the graph edge.

4. Root cause

  • Graph edge: the vol→PK / correlation / graphical-LASSO edges mostly re-encode a common market factor that is already a node feature (market_pk); conditional on it, no edge beats HAR or the graph-removed model out-of-sample (it even significantly hurts at h10).
  • News: removing the news branch significantly lowers QLIKE at h1–h5 (news does not help under this architecture/data); its signal is weak/noisy at daily frequency and overlaps price history.
  • Where value is: node features (market factor + volume z-score) read through a non-linear LSTM give a small, significant edge over a same-feature linear model (HAR-X) at h1 and h5; the linear HAR family remains the standard to beat at h10–h22.
